# Data Subject Rights (DSR) Procedure — GDPR Art. 15-22

## Request Types

| Right | Article | Response Time |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Access | Art. 15 | 30 days |
| Rectification | Art. 16 | 30 days |
| Erasure | Art. 17 | 30 days |
| Portability | Art. 20 | 30 days |

## Erasure Limitations

Data that **cannot** be erased due to legal obligations:
- Financial records (7-year tax retention)
- PCI audit logs (12-month PCI DSS retention)
- Active transaction records

## Procedure

1. Receive request via DSR form or email
2. Verify identity of requester
3. Identify all data held for the subject
4. Separate erasable data from legally retained data
5. Execute erasure for eligible data
6. Respond within 30 days with summary of actions taken
