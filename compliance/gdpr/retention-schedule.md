# Retention Schedule

| Data Category | Retention | Legal Basis | Review |
|--------------|-----------|-------------|--------|
| Invoices / VAT records | 7 years | LT accounting law | Annual |
| Payment transaction records | 7 years | LT/LV/EE tax law | Annual |
| PCI audit logs | 12 months | PCI DSS Req. 10.5.1 | Quarterly |
| Customer account data | Account lifetime | GDPR Art. 17 | On request |
| Session data | Browser session | Technical necessity | N/A |
| Failed payment attempts | 30 days | Operational | Monthly |
