# Lawful Basis — GDPR Art. 6(1)(b) Contractual Necessity

## Payment Detail Processing

Processing payment details is necessary for performance of a contract under Article 6(1)(b) GDPR.

The ICO explicitly cites this as covering:
- Address processing for delivery
- Payment detail processing for fulfilling purchase obligations

## Scope

| Processing Activity | Lawful Basis | Justification |
|-------------------|-------------|---------------|
| Collecting delivery address | Art. 6(1)(b) | Necessary to fulfill order |
| Processing payment via Stripe | Art. 6(1)(b) | Necessary to receive payment |
| Generating VAT invoice | Art. 6(1)(c) | Legal obligation (tax law) |
| Sending order confirmation | Art. 6(1)(b) | Necessary to confirm contract performance |
| Marketing communications | Art. 6(1)(a) | Separate consent required |
| Profiling / preferences | N/A | Not currently performed |

## Important Notes

- Profiling and preference analysis require a **separate** lawful basis
- Marketing requires explicit consent (Art. 6(1)(a))
- Payment processing under Art. 6(1)(b) does NOT extend to analytics

Source: [ICO Lawful Basis Guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/lawful-basis/a-guide-to-lawful-basis/contract/)
